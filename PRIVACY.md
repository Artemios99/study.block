# Privacy Policy — Study Blocker

Study Blocker does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal user data to third parties.

## What data the extension accesses

The extension checks the URL of your active browser tab to determine whether it matches a website you have chosen to block during a focus session. This check happens locally, in real time, and the URL is not saved, logged, or sent anywhere.

## What data the extension stores

The following information is stored locally on your device only, using Chrome's local storage:

- The list of websites you choose to block
- Your current focus session state (active/inactive, timer)
- Your focus time statistics (daily totals, used to show Today/Week/Month/All-time stats)

None of this data ever leaves your device. It is not transmitted to any server, and the developer has no access to it.

## Third parties

This extension does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
